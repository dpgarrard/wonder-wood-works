Product Questions.

What products do we make?

Our products vary. We can build signage, small and large furniture, and furniture accessories.

How are we different from the competition?

Every piece of furniture is either drafted using CAD software, or hand drawn and made without the aid of computers. The type of product wanted determines which route we take. Additionally, we can make custom tailored designs and furniture to suit our customers needs.

What is the average turn around for small and large orders?

Most small orders have a 1-2 week turn around depending on if its a custom or standard piece. Most large orders are typically 1 month depending on the actual size of orders. specify any distinguishing feature in the order info page under additional information.
